Typing guide打字规则

Your first send

Open the demo chat, tap h and i, then press Aim. Hit the target to fill the chat field with hi. Tap the chat’s Send button to post it.

进入示例会话，在草稿框输入 hi，点判定。命中后文字填入上方聊天框，再点聊天框旁的发送。

Edit the draft · 编辑草稿

Tap between two characters in the keyboard draft to place the caret. New keys insert there, keeping the text on either side. Swipe across a long draft to scroll it.

点按草稿中的字符间隙定位光标，后续输入会插在这里。长草稿可横向滑动。

Type 输入与余量

In the seven collision modes, tap moving keys to build a draft. Each letter and digit starts with 3 uses. The small number on its key counts down. The last tap still writes the character.

七款碰撞键盘中，每个字母和数字起始各有 3 次。键帽角标显示余量，最后一次照常写入。

Space is unlimited. Shift changes case and uses the same balance. Sending requires at least one visible character.

空格不限次数，大小写共用余量。纯空格草稿无法发送。

Delete a word 三连删词

Place the caret just after three identical letters or digits to unlock Delete. Delete removes the whole word containing that triple. Surrounding spaces and other words stay in place.

光标左侧紧邻三个相同字母或数字时，解锁删除。点删除会移除三连所在的整词，保留两侧空格和其他单词。删除不返还次数。
go baaa → ⌫ → go␠ ␠ 表示保留的空格。若最后一个 a 恰好耗尽，可先删词再补 A，也可保留草稿直接换弹。

Reload 换弹与广告

Tap the reload prompt for the spent key. After the demo, tap the slot once per animation. Each success earns one use. Bank uses returns everything earned to that key.

点耗尽字符的换弹入口。等演示结束，每次动画接受一次操作。成功一发得一次，点「完成换弹」结算，单键余量无上限。
100% → 75% → 56.25% → …

Every new reload starts at 100%. A jam opens one of three ads. Earlier successes are kept.

每次新回合从必成开始，之后成功率逐次乘 ¾。失败时随机出现一则广告，已经赢得的次数保留。

Ad exits · Wait for the countdown or clip to finish. Tap Claim on the first ad, the red mark below the title on War Thunder, or the left Play button on Boom Beach.

倒计时或视频结束后，分别点「待查收」、战争雷霆标题下的红色标志、海岛奇兵左侧的「点击即玩」。外跳按钮会打开对应网页，返回后仍需在广告内领取。

Aim & send 指针判定

Tap the dial when the needle touches the target arc. A miss moves and shortens the arc. The clock drains 2× as fast and the needle moves 1.5× as fast after each miss.

按下瞬间判定。未命中会换到更短的新目标弧，剩余时间继续流逝，两种加速逐次叠加。

When time runs out, retry with a fresh clock and target. A hit inserts the draft at the active app’s selection. Tap Send in that app when ready. Your remaining key uses carry over.

超时后重开一轮，时间与速度重置。命中后草稿填入应用输入框，点应用的发送键才会发出。字符余量延续到下一条。

Alternate modes 另外两种玩法

Chalk · 简约纯白

Tap or glide across letters, then pick a suggestion. Delete a character or a whole word and undo a word deletion. Aim opens the dial. A completed reload has its own sponsor screen. The timeout screen also offers Insert anyway.

支持点按、滑行和候选词。整词删除无需三连，可短时撤销。点右下角 Aim 进入判定；完成换弹会有专属广告，超时可选继续填入。

Phantom · 神秘风

Tap the draft to position the caret. Used keys dim and lock. Reload restores a random used character on success. Pause or adjust the key speed to aim. Backspace clears the entire draft, as does a lost send round.

点按草稿定位光标，用过的字符会锁定；换弹成功随机恢复一个。可暂停或调速，退格和发送判定失败都会清空整条草稿。
